Model merging combines domain-specialized experts trained from a shared foundation model into one multi-task model. This work introduces Dual-Tuned Experts (DTEs), a two-stage strategy that first learns prompts and then fine-tunes the vision encoder. Across multiple CLIP architectures and both full fine-tuning and LoRA experts, DTEs improve the performance of standard model-merging methods and remain effective for heterogeneous experts.
